Dementia alone costs €185bn yearly. A 2-year delay in onset saves ≈ €55bn / year by 2050.

Families can save money. 58% of dementia cost, >€100bn, is not covered by public budget.

Fill the skill gap. In Italy, 73% of 65–74s are connected and 27% are skilled. France and Spain show the same 40–50 point gap. AI closes it with zero-skills UX.

Convergence is fast: voice, vision and agents were lab demos in 2023. Frontier accuracy on Humanity's Last Exam went 9% → 50% in 15 months.

Free time is opportunity. 65+ watch TV ~2.8 hours a day, 40% of their free time. AI brings active contents, stimulation, memory and family contact.

Value accrues here

Context is the key moat. Switching cost grows with every interaction as data compounds. A competitor cannot match the experience on day one.

Compliance lives in the router. Consent, EU data residency and logging are enforced once, for every model behind it. New rules land in one place.

No supplier lock-in. Models are interchangeable and a swap takes days. A supplier outage triggers automatic fallback. The user sees only the improvement.
