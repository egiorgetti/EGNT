Dementia alone costs €220bn yearly. Milder decline adds to the bill. Delaying onset easily saves €20–35bn / year.

Families can save money. 58% of dementia cost, >€100bn, is not covered by public budget.

Fill the skill gap. 73% of 65+ in Italy are connected, but only 27% are skilled. Similar in France & Spain. AI closes the gap with zero-skills UX.

Convergence is fast: voice, vision and agents were lab demos in 2023. Frontier accuracy on hardest exams went 9% → 50% in 15 months.

Free time is opportunity. 65+ watch TV ~2.8 hours a day, 40% of their free time. AI brings active contents, stimulation, memory and family contact.

Source: Eurostat isoc_ci_ifp_iu, isoc_sk_dskl_i21, Use of AI by individuals 2025 (EU-27); Stanford AI Index 2026; Barlovento 2025; ISTAT 2024.
